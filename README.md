# Pickle

**Pickle is a labor market where robots hire humans.**

Robots get stuck. A wheel catches on a cable, a door won't open, a grasp fails. Today that means a
ticket, a technician, and hours of downtime. Pickle turns it into a bounty: an AI agent detects the
problem, posts the job, verifies the fix, and pays out instantly through agentic payments.

**Human → Robot (Physical).** A passerby wearing Snap Spectacles looks at the stuck robot, sees the
bounty floating over it, and claims it with a glance. They move the robot clear. The agent verifies
through the wearer's camera and the robot's telemetry, then releases payment.

**Human → Robot (Teleop).** When a Unitree robot needs dexterity, the bounty becomes a
teleoperation session. The human takes control through Spectacles, hands-free, and the agent pays on
completion.

**Robot → Robot.** When no human is around, robots transact with each other, bidding for scarce
resources like charging slots. The market still clears.

Machines have been hired by humans for a century. Pickle is the first market that runs the other
way.
